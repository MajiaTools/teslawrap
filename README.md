## What it does

- **8,393+ published Tesla wrap designs** in a searchable gallery — filter by model or category, open any compatible template without an account
- **Model-specific canvases** for Tesla Model 3, Model Y, Model S, Model X, and Cybertruck, including year- and trim-specific layouts so the preview matches your exact vehicle
- **Browser preview** of a custom Tesla wrap in 2D or live 3D before you work on your own design
- **Local sticker editor** — add your own images without sending the files or design settings to our servers
- **Model-specific export checker** — verifies PNG format, dimensions, file size, and filename before the texture leaves your device
- Wrap planning tips and an FAQ covering Tesla Paint Shop workflows and digital vs physical wraps

## How to use

1. Open https://teslawrap.io and choose your exact Tesla model and year
2. Search the gallery and open a compatible wrap design in the browser preview
3. Inspect the layout in 2D or 3D, then use the export checker before transferring the PNG to Paint Shop
